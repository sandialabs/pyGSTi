# Operators

Models are assembled from operation, state-preparation, and measurement objects. [Operator types and parameterizations](OperatorTypes) demonstrates the built-in classes, Lindblad parameterizations, and composition and embedding of smaller operators.

- [Custom operators](CustomOperators) defines new operation classes and factories.
- [Custom POVMs](CustomPOVMs) constructs a measurement beyond the built-in choices.
- [Parameter labels, bounds, and tying](TyingParameters) accesses individual parameters and constrains or shares them.

For constructing a complete device model, start with [Defining models](../../guides/models/DeviceModels).
