# Bases and state spaces

A model's matrices depend on both its state space and its choice of basis. These notebooks explain the conventions and the objects that represent them:

- [Working with bases](UsingBases) constructs matrix bases and changes the basis of operation matrices and state-preparation and measurement vectors.
- [State spaces](StateSpaces) describes subsystem labels, dimensions, tensor products, and direct sums.

For a first introduction to the matrix representation of a quantum operation, see [Key concepts](../../start/KeyConcepts).
